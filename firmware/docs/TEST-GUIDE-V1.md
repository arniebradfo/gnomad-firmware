# Gnomad V1: what to test, and where

For V1.6 and later. Two modes; switch either way by holding **HOME + SAVE for 2 seconds**.
**Power-on brings back the last thing you saved, in the mode you saved it from, stopped.**

## Easy (the pictures, the Gnome)

1. **Sounds.** Turn ALGORITHM for a picture, PRESETS for the sound inside it. Play the keys. KNOB 1-4: Bright, Wobble, Echo, Space.
2. **Grooves.** SEQ (the loop page: a block a bar), then turn PRESETS: five grooves load and play. PLAY/STOP stops them.
3. **Record a loop.** REC, then PLAY: it counts 4 3 2 1, play keys, REC again to stop. SAVE held = undo the last recording.
4. **Metronome.** Hold EDIT and turn KNOB 3: right ON, left OFF. It clicks only while recording or counting in (silent while the loop just plays: by design).
5. **Save.** Tap SAVE: the Gnome says SAVED. Or just stop and wait 4 s: Easy saves by itself (a check mark by the tempo).
6. **Power off, on.** Easy comes back with that song, stopped. Press PLAY.

## Advanced (Felucca's pages, Gnomad colours)

1. **Switch.** Hold HOME + SAVE 2 s: Advanced.
2. **Build a beat.** Felucca's pages as before (SEQ steps, engines, FX).
3. **Shape a sound.** ENV, LFO (with Wobble up), FX > DLY / REVERB / CHORUS, and EDIT 1 (the sound's four knobs) / EDIT 2
   (four more of the engine's own) change the Gnomad sounds. A knob that has nothing to do on that sound says why in words
   ("Always a sine", "Knob 3 has it"). No pan: the sound engine is mono.
4. **Record.** Felucca's recording clicks too, with the same metronome setting as Easy.
5. **Save.** SAVE button, PROJECT page: KNOB 1 the slot (1-4), turn KNOB 4 right (SAVE), OCT+, name it, OCT+. SAVED.
6. **Load.** Same page: KNOB 1 the slot, turn KNOB 3 right (LOAD), OCT+.
7. **Power off, on.** Advanced comes back with that project loaded (LOADED), stopped.

## After power off and on

| Your last save was | The synth starts |
| --- | --- |
| in Easy (SAVE or its autosave) | Easy, that song, stopped |
| in Advanced (SAVE or a rename) | Advanced, that project, stopped |
| nothing saved yet | the mode you left it in |

Easy's autosave writes only its own song's slot or an empty one, never over a save made in Advanced (or by the app).
All four slots full: it says FULL and keeps nothing. Make room by saving over a slot you do not need in Advanced.
